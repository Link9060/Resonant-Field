# ADR 0001 — Field is a shared platform module

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Field will be developed as its own Resonant Assist engineering project/repository, but it will not be positioned as an independent consumer product.

## Consequences

- Relay, RAVIN, and future apps can reuse the same knowledge contracts.
- Field can have its own release/versioning lifecycle.
- Field Explorer may exist as an internal development UI.
- Consumer-facing experiences remain inside their respective products.
